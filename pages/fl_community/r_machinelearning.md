# Reddit: r/MachineLearning

- **URL:** [https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/)
- **Description:** A broad machine learning subreddit with frequent federated learning (FL) discussions, such as threads on why FL isn’t more mainstream. It’s a large community with diverse ML topics.
- **Access Details:** Open, requires a Reddit account to participate.
- **Focus:** General ML with FL as a subset; practical and theoretical discussions.
- **Interaction Style:** Asynchronous, thread-based discussions.
- **Additional Notes:** Active with over 2 million members; FL threads may require searching but are valuable for broader context.

## What is the EasyFL Tool Assembly?

[EasyFL](https://github.com/THU-BPM/EasyFL) is an open-source, low-code federated learning platform designed for experimentation and prototyping. It supports heterogeneity simulation, training flow abstraction, and distributed training optimization, making it suitable for users with varying expertise levels. EasyFL is user-friendly and deployable for both research and industrial application prototypes.

### What can you use the EasyFL Tool Assembly for?
- User-friendly FL experimentation and prototyping
- Heterogeneity simulation and training flow abstraction
- Distributed training optimization

### Limitations/Remarks
- Focused on experimentation and prototyping
- Ongoing development and feature expansion

### Specific Use Cases/Applications
- Research and industrial FL application prototypes
- Rapid experimentation with FL algorithms

### How to access the EasyFL Tool Assembly?
- [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/THU-BPM/EasyFL)
- Install via pip or from source

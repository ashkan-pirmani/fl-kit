## What is the MetisFL Tool Assembly?

[MetisFL](https://github.com/awslabs/metisfl) is an open-source federated learning framework designed for modularity, extensibility, and configurability. It supports both TensorFlow and PyTorch backends and is intended for general-purpose FL across a variety of domains, including computer vision and natural language processing. MetisFL is domain-agnostic and can be extended to support additional ML frameworks.

### What can you use the MetisFL Tool Assembly for?
- General-purpose federated learning research and applications
- Cross-domain FL in CV, NLP, and more
- Extensible backend support (TensorFlow, PyTorch)

### Limitations/Remarks
- May face challenges with very specific or niche requirements
- Ongoing development and optimization

### Specific Use Cases/Applications
- FL in computer vision, NLP, and other domains
- Research and prototyping of new FL algorithms

### How to access the MetisFL Tool Assembly?
- [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/awslabs/metisfl)
- Install via pip or from source

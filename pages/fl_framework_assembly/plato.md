## What is the Plato Tool Assembly?

[Plato](https://plato-research.org/) is an open-source federated learning research framework. It is designed for scalable and reproducible FL research, supporting a wide range of FL algorithms and real-world system implementations. Plato is extensible and suitable for deployment in various environments, making it a valuable tool for both academic and industrial FL research.

### What can you use the Plato Tool Assembly for?
- Scalable and reproducible federated learning research
- Deployment of FL algorithms in diverse environments
- Extensible FL experimentation

### Limitations/Remarks
- Focused on research and experimentation
- Ongoing development and optimization

### Specific Use Cases/Applications
- Academic and industrial FL research
- Deployment of FL in real-world systems

### How to access the Plato Tool Assembly?
- [Official Documentation](https://plato-research.org/)
- [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/TL-System/Plato)
- Install via pip or from source

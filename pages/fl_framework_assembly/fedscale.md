## What is the FedScale Tool Assembly?

[FedScale](https://fedscale.ai/) is an open-source federated learning benchmark and runtime platform. It offers realistic FL tasks, scalable runtime, and diverse datasets, focusing on benchmarking model and system performance at scale. FedScale is designed to address client heterogeneity and large-scale FL deployment challenges, facilitating reproducible FL research across domains such as image classification and speech recognition.

### What can you use the FedScale Tool Assembly for?
- Benchmarking federated learning models and systems
- Heterogeneity-aware FL research
- Large-scale FL deployment simulation

### Limitations/Remarks
- Focused on benchmarking and research
- Ongoing development and feature expansion

### Specific Use Cases/Applications
- Image classification, speech recognition, and other FL domains
- Reproducible FL research and benchmarking

## What is the FLSim Tool Assembly?

[FLSim](https://github.com/facebookresearch/FLSim) is an open-source, domain-agnostic federated learning simulation framework. It is designed for scalability and supports differential privacy, secure aggregation, and compression techniques. FLSim provides a flexible and comprehensive simulation environment for exploring various aspects of federated learning, making it suitable for research and development in cross-device FL.

### What can you use the FLSim Tool Assembly for?
- Simulation of federated learning environments
- Research in differential privacy, secure aggregation, and compression
- Scalable FL experimentation

### Limitations/Remarks
- Focused on simulation and research
- Ongoing development and feature expansion

### Specific Use Cases/Applications
- Building simulators for FL research
- Exploring cross-device FL scenarios

### How to access the FLSim Tool Assembly?
- [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/facebookresearch/FLSim)
- Install via pip or from source
